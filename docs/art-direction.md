# Art direction · 审美方向

## Recognizable subject, open paper

The image must retain its central identity before it gains texture. Preserve structure, pose, proportion, important color, and meaningful relationships. Reduce surrounding clutter rather than shrink the photograph into a rectangular sticker.

The portrait default is 3:4, with roughly 55–65% quiet warm paper as a starting point. This is a composition target, not a measured mask. Move and scale the subject according to its shape; preserve the internal symmetry of symmetric architecture.

## A shallow, interrupted transition

The photographic core, the material transition, and the quiet sheet have different jobs. Soft fiber ridges and thin torn sheets appear at a few openings. Real detail may extend into a fibrous area, while another edge fades gently. Avoid a closed torn-paper ring or a uniform relief filter across the whole scene.

## Pale, scarce, spacious pigment

Start with the adjacent subject's color. Let a diluted stain catch in a fiber bundle, feather into nearby paper, then disappear. Most pulp remains white. Independent droplets are optional and rare; a deep string of dots, saturated splash field, or large color cloud is a failure of hierarchy, even if it is decorative.

Increase absorption character before increasing color quantity. Preserve the photographic core and existing material height when revising pigment.

## A title rooted in the picture

Compare 3–5 candidates from different visual ideas: a relationship, a shape or color, a gesture, or a concrete local detail. Prefer natural, concise Chinese with rhythm and room for interpretation. Reject invented weather, season, identity, history, and strained wording. Four characters are useful, not compulsory.

A small English interpretation follows the selected Chinese meaning. Lock all wording before drawing it. Light Song/Ming lettering, much smaller English, one short source-color line, and sparse contextual text organize the blank space. A correctly spelled heavy headline is still a typography mismatch.

## 中文要点

- 先保主体与空间，再安排纸浆；不要靠满铺肌理掩盖结构偏差。
- 交界有断有续：真实细节、纤维、薄片、积色与露白彼此穿插，不围成整齐一圈。
- 颜料默认浅、少、疏，按相邻景物取色。染色处仍透出纸色，色痕抢主体时先减淡、减量。
- 题名从画面关系与形色出发，兼顾贴图、自然、节奏、古雅余味；不以生僻字和虚构故事添“古风”。
- 文字准确与排版轻盈分别检查；修一个问题时继承已经通过的部分。
